# Forage-KPMG-Audit-Assessment
on Fitness LLC Client
In order to identify which accounts are risky and require additional audit procedures, you will need opt understand the context of the Globo Gym business environment. This information is provided by a detailed discussion with the Chief Financial Officer (CFO), who will reveal key areas in the business that may create financial statement risk.

Audits are designed to provide an opinion of the financial statements as a whole, but generally do this by focusing on accounts that are “material”, meaning their magnitude or nature is significant enough that their misstatement or omission could influence the economic decisions of users of the financial statements. The latest accounts of the company are provided below.

You will use both the management discussion of the business as well as pre-defined materiality thresholds to help create a risk rating for individual accounts.  

Material Account. What is it?
An account whose magnitude or nature is significant enough that its misstatement or omission could influence the economic decisions of users of the financial statements.
